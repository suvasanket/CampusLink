## Core System Summary

CampusLink is an explainable campus placement intelligence engine:
1. **Deterministic Eligibility:** Filter candidate pool strictly by CGPA, branch, and backlogs.
2. **Semantic Matching:** Calculate embedding and taxonomy-based similarity for technical skills and projects.
3. **Multi-Factor Scoring:** Weighted score across Skills (40%), Projects (20%), Academics (15%), Assessment (10%), Certifications (10%), and Communication (5%).
4. **Explainable Rationale:** Deterministic breakdown + grounded natural language summary.
5. **Skill Gap Diagnostics:** Separate required vs preferred gaps with readiness tiers.
